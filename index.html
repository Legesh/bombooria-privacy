Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bombooria ("the Game"), developed and published by its individual developer ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you play the Game.

1. Information We Handle

The Game may store gameplay progress and settings locally on your device so that you can continue playing.

Depending on the services enabled in the version you use, technical information may be processed by third-party providers that support advertising, analytics, diagnostics or other game functionality.

The exact information processed depends on the providers and their configuration. Please refer to the relevant provider privacy policies for further details.

2. Advertising

The Game may display optional rewarded advertisements and other advertisements.

Advertising providers may process technical information to deliver, measure and prevent fraud in advertisements. The information and purposes depend on the advertising services integrated into the version of the Game.

3. Analytics and Diagnostics

The Game may use analytics or crash-reporting services to understand performance, identify errors and improve the player experience.

These services, if enabled, may process technical information about the app, device or gameplay events.

4. In-App Purchases

The Game may offer optional digital purchases. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Purchases are subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policy: Apple Privacy Policy .

5. Third-Party Services

Some Game functionality may rely on third-party service providers. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

The final version of this policy will identify the providers actually integrated into the released Game.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally can generally be removed by deleting the Game or clearing its local data. Information processed by third-party providers is subject to their retention and deletion policies.

You may contact us with privacy-related questions or requests using the contact details provided below.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

Where applicable, advertising and data-processing services must be configured to comply with relevant children's privacy laws and platform requirements.

8. International Users

Third-party service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Such processing is subject to applicable laws and the providers' privacy practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the Game, please contact the developer using the support contact provided on the Game's App Store product page.

Game Support

Need help with Bombooria, found a bug or have an idea that could make the game better?

Please use the support contact listed on the Bombooria App Store product page.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include:

  • Your device model.
  • Your iOS version.
  • The Game version.
  • A short description of what happened.

Please do not include passwords, payment card details or other sensitive personal information in support requests.
